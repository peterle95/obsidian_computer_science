## 🚀 JavaScript
```dataview
TABLE last-reviewed, language
FROM "Computer Science"
WHERE contains(tags, "learning") OR contains(tags, "learned") OR contains(tags, "to_learn") OR contains(tags, "will_learn") AND  contains(language, "JavaScript")
SORT last-reviewed ASC
```
